Help / Tips
  • Pick a sound, then tap Float over camera and open your camera app. Tap the floating video to reveal its controls.
  • If floating video is unavailable, try then press Home. Support depends on your browser and phone; the on-page controls always remain available.
  • System media controls (lock screen / PiP controls) map to: Play = play the selected sound, Pause = stop, Next = switch sound and play. Start loop on this page when you want repeats. Some browsers omit Next.
  • Desktop shortcuts: Space play once, N next sound, L loop, S stop, P PiP, F fullscreen.
Move the floating player close to your camera lens. Play a sound when you are ready to take the photo.
